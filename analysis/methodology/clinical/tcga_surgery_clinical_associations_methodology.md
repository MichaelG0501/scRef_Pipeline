## Variables and statistical decisions

The selected priority figures cover sex, age at diagnosis (>60 versus <=60), grade (G1/G2 versus G3/G4), and pathological AJCC8 stage (1–4). Supporting candidates are clinical stage, clinical T/N, pathological T, pathological nodal involvement (N0 versus N1–3), M classification, positive-node burden (0, 1–3, >=4), location, alcohol history, smoking exposure (0, >0–20, >20 pack-years), prior malignancy/treatment, recorded race/ethnicity and preoperative-treatment recording. These variables reflect demographic context, tumour extent/differentiation and exposure/treatment context; administrative identifiers, sequencing annotations, follow-up duration and death status are not indiscriminately screened as clinical boxplot groups. Race and ethnicity are source-recorded descriptors, not biological causal variables.

Blank/unknown/not-available and Tx/Nx/Mx/Gx values are missing. T substages reduce to T0–T4; clinical N uses its explicit category, while pathological N uses the prespecified N0/N+ grouping to retain node-positive patients with sparse high-N levels. Grade and smoking thresholds are prespecified rather than optimized for association. Positive-node burden overlaps conceptually with pathological N and is secondary evidence, not independent replication. Missing smoking values do not imply no exposure. Sparse metadata remain in the inventory with counts and exclusion reasons.

Each retained category requires at least ten unique patients, matching the OCCAMS clinical-association rule. Each variable needs at least two retained categories; other categories are excluded only for that comparison. Thus the displayed N may be below 72. With two groups use a two-sided unpaired Wilcoxon rank-sum test (`exact=FALSE`); with more groups use Kruskal–Wallis. Test all eligible signatures and adjust p-values by BH separately within each clinical variable and signature family (17, five, five). Star thresholds use adjusted p <0.05, <0.01 and <0.001. Test failures/nonfinite results stop execution. These are exploratory unadjusted associations; per-family BH does not control one global error rate across all clinical variables. Bulk RNA composition, clinical confounding, incomplete treatment histories and surgical selection limit interpretation. Surgery-Yes does not establish documented absence of preoperative treatment.
