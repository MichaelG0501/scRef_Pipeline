# All-patient malignant OS meta-analysis

## Scope and input authority

`analysis/clinical/malignant_survival_meta_analysis.R` uses every patient in the existing malignant-expression parent populations: 281 OCCAMS, 88 TCGA EAC, 110 GSE207526 and 52 GSE19417 EAC specimens. The current default includes all four datasets; SCREF_INCLUDE_GEO=FALSE explicitly requests RNA-seq-only sensitivity analysis. No treatment, surgery, specimen or Mandard subgroup restriction is applied. One patient is the statistical unit. The statistical synthesis pools independent dataset estimates, rather than concatenating patients into a single Cox model. Dataset independence is assumed from their distinct recruitment sources; patient-level cross-study identity cannot be verified from public anonymised identifiers.

The current OCCAMS September 25 corrected workbook supplies the curated endpoint and patient fields. The September 18 TCGA workbook supplies patient identity and selection; explicitly recorded cBioPortal fields supply OS where absent from that workbook. GSE uses the original supplied clinical file curated by its maintained metadata producer. Their exact paths and hashes are validated through the two producer input manifests before fitting. No metadata are imputed from an older source.

The existing Gaussian GSVA scores and five within-malignant RNA fractions are reused. The final 17 MPs, five MP-union signatures, five ranked-marker DGE signatures and five fractions define a fixed 32-feature family. Excluded MPs remain excluded. Scores are not recalculated on endpoint-complete subsets. The datasets have separately defined retained gene universes; common feature identity does not imply identical gene coverage. Existing coverage tables remain authoritative. MP12 and the stress-adaptive MP union may represent the same gene set and cannot count as separate validation.

## Patient models

A patient enters a fit only with finite positive recorded OS time and explicit event code 0 or 1. All input patients remain in the model-source table, including exclusions. Current recorded OS origins are preserved, not asserted equivalent. Alive/not recorded dead is treated as censored under the source OCCAMS convention. Time-unit changes do not alter Cox HRs, but different time origins and informative missingness remain scientific limitations.

Continuous expression uses one SD among endpoint-eligible patients within each dataset. Fractions use ten percentage points. All models are unadjusted, following the requested all-patient comparison. Efron ties are used. Continuous models require 20 patients and ten events. Binary models require 20 patients, five events overall, at least five patients per arm and an event in each arm. These match existing comparator feasibility rules; failures remain explicit and are never silently dropped from meta-analysis. Unexpected numerical warnings stop execution. Fixed Cox tolerances are eps=1e-12, toler.chol=1e-14 and 50 iterations. Continuous feature PH tests are tabulated.

Median models contrast values above the dataset median with values at or below it. Quartile models contrast values at or above Q3 with values at or below Q1; middle patients are excluded only from that model. Tied quartiles are explicitly unsupported. Cutpoints use R's default type-7 quantiles. Optimal-cut models search the same 13 quantiles from 0.20 through 0.80 in 0.05 steps; corrected P is min(1,13 times the minimum eligible raw Wald P). All candidates are retained. These selected HRs and SEs are biased by outcome-based selection, so they are displayed as exploratory dataset results and are not pooled.

## Statistical synthesis

For each continuous, median or quartile feature specification, all configured dataset estimates must be available. An unsupported required estimate produces an explicit unestimated pooled row; there is no automatic substitution by a smaller dataset set.

Random-effects tau-squared is estimated by restricted maximum likelihood. The scalar restricted negative log-likelihood, ignoring constants, is one half of the sum of log(v_i+tau2), log(sum(w_i)) and sum(w_i*(y_i-mu)^2), with w_i=1/(v_i+tau2) and mu the weighted mean. The nonnegative boundary is evaluated explicitly. Optimisation stops if its predetermined conservative upper bound is approached. Modified Hartung–Knapp uses SE=sqrt(max(1,sum(w_i*(y_i-mu)^2)/(k-1))/sum(w_i)); tests and intervals use Student's t with k-1 degrees of freedom. This prevents adjusted intervals from becoming narrower than ordinary random-effects intervals. This is the documented `test="adhoc"` procedure in [metafor](https://wviechtb.github.io/metafor/reference/rma.uni.html), implemented directly without an extra package dependency.

Cochran's Q, its P value, REML tau-squared and I-squared are reported. I-squared uses REML tau-squared divided by tau-squared plus the typical within-study variance, following the metafor definition. With few studies these quantities are imprecise. Leave-one-dataset-out analyses use the same model with k-1 estimates and are sensitivity analyses, not independent evidence. Direction agreement and nominal P<0.05 in every configured dataset are recorded separately from pooled significance. Pooled significance alone does not establish replication.

BH correction uses 32 features within each dataset/method and separately within each pooled method. Missing tests remain in the fixed family size. The correction does not cover choosing among methods or additional investigations. Continuous results are primary; other splits are exploratory. No signature is selected or excluded because it agrees with an expected direction.

## GEO microarray assessment

The legacy integrated analysis used GSE19417, an Agilent two-colour study. GEO explicitly defines VALUE as normalised log10(Cy5/Cy3), tumour/reference: [sample record](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM482796). Exponentiating these values yields ratios, not absolute tumour abundance; a gene-specific reference intensity is still missing. Those ratios must not be relabelled RNA-seq counts or absolute malignant expression. BayesPrism's developers describe microarray applications as experimental and recommend checking technical differences and ground truth: [developer discussion](https://github.com/Danko-Lab/BayesPrism/issues/96). Microarray deconvolution is possible in principle, including CIBERSORTx, but requires appropriate linear input and validation.

The original sample-level assessment is retained in `ref_outs/clinical_malignant_meta/reference/`. Maintained downloads, processing, exploratory deconvolution, scoring and QC now run under `analysis/GEO/`, with persistent outputs under `ref_outs/GEO/`. The original supplementary INTENSITY2 tumour channel is available and preparation/inference completed for all 52 EAC specimens, of which 48 have usable OS. Raw processed ratios are never substituted for malignant expression. The current default synthesis includes GEO; leave-one-out results excluding GSE19417 provide RNA-seq-only sensitivity estimates. GSE13898 has no public patient-level survival in the existing inventory and was not used by the legacy integrated analysis.

## Figures, reproducibility and validation

Two final PDFs show MPs and states separately, three pages each: continuous, median and Q1/Q4. Each page compares all configured datasets and their pooled estimate using shared log HR axes zoomed equally for every dataset and pooled column, full MP descriptions, dataset colours, open/filled/diamond evidence symbols and concise n/event headers. Intervals beyond the plotting range have arrowheads; full intervals remain in tables. Unsupported estimates are labelled NE. The separate two-page optimal-cut PDF contains no pooled column. No explanatory subtitles or captions are added.

Persistent cache contracts include scientific input hashes, source-manifest validation, settings, package versions and statistical function bodies. A changed contract stops; `SCREF_FORCE_REBUILD=TRUE` explicitly rebuilds. `SCREF_REPLOT_ONLY=TRUE` requires a matching cache. All reproducible source tables and caches remain under the live project output root. PBS uses dmtcp, one CPU, 8 GB and one hour; matching launcher is `job_scripts/clinical/malignant_survival_meta_analysis.sh`. No temporary audit is a production dependency.

Validation checks every parent patient and feature, source alignment, model counts and multiplicity; independently refits representative Cox models and verifies all pooled calculations. PDF validation checks all pages, text bounds, descriptions, evidence labels and expected panel counts. Representative MP/state pages are rendered and inspected before claiming publication readiness.

## Explicit microarray-inclusive execution

SCREF_INCLUDE_GEO=TRUE adds all 52 GSE19417 EAC specimens to the configured parent populations and requires the GEO scoring source tables and matching manifest. Missing files stop execution; no three-dataset substitution occurs. The default TRUE includes GEO; setting FALSE explicitly runs the three RNA-seq studies alone. Changed dataset configuration requires SCREF_FORCE_REBUILD=TRUE. All configured datasets are required for each pooled estimate, with k-1 degrees of freedom. Main pooled outputs use the explicitly configured set; when GEO is included, the leave-one-out rows excluding GSE19417 reproduce the RNA-seq-only sensitivity synthesis. GEO results remain exploratory because of cross-platform uncertainty. No GEO specimen is selected by treatment or survival direction.

## Current completed QC

The four-dataset analysis includes 523 OS-eligible patients and 319 deaths (OCCAMS 280/189; TCGA 88/45; GSE207526 107/46; GSE19417 48/39). GEO inference retained 13,102 genes; all 27 expression signatures retained at least five genes. Squamous-to-intestinal fractions are below 0.1% in 51/52 GEO specimens, glandular-to-intestinal in 45/52, and immune-mimicry in 41/52. These states are poorly resolved by this microarray transfer; their fractions must not be presented as validated cell proportions. No signature or specimen was removed on this basis. Cross-platform gene-coverage differences and uncertain deconvolution preclude interpreting pooled numerical checks as biological validation. All 96 full meta-analysis estimates passed independent metafor comparisons; maximum discrepancy was 1.14e-8. No continuous, median or quartile pooled association reaches nominal P<0.05 under the specified modified Hartung–Knapp model.

## Forest-plot zoom and effect units

All three PDFs use a 20-by-12-inch page without adding pages. Every dataset and pooled column shares the same axis on each page, including all state-feature families. Continuous pages use a symmetric log2-HR display from 0.5 to 2; median, quartile and optimal-cut pages use 0.25 to 4. These display limits do not filter estimates. Boundary arrows identify intervals beyond the display; exact estimates and intervals remain in tables. Matching scales permit direct comparison of horizontal distances between dataset and pooled columns. No statistical model or effect scale changes in this replot.

Expression effects are labelled per one score SD; the SD is dataset-specific among endpoint-eligible patients. State-fraction effects are labelled per ten percentage points, for example an increase from 20% to 30%. Ten percentage points is not assumed equal to one SD. The MP page states its unit on the x axis; continuous state pages identify the expression and fraction units in their family strips. Median/quartile/optimal-cut models remain high-versus-low group comparisons. The neutral hazard ratio is one; zero is the neutral log-hazard ratio.
