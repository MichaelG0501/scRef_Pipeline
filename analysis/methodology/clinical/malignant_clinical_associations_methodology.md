# Malignant-compartment clinical associations

## Scope and metadata authority

`analysis/clinical/Auto_malignant_clinical_associations.R` is the single producer for both cohorts. The unit of analysis is one patient with one explicitly mapped bulk RNA sample. Metadata are read directly from the corrected OCCAMS workbook dated 25 September 2026 (`Patient_Master_388`) and TCGA workbook dated 18 September 2026 (`EAC_RNA88_Final`). These are the explicit current curated releases; filenames are not selected by modification time. All patient, sample and cohort joins use exact identifiers.

Multiple metadata sources are necessary. The OCCAMS workbook supplies all covered fields. Treatment-plan comorbidity comes only from `ref_outs/OCCAMS/clinical/tables/Auto_OCCAMS_qc_pass_subject_metadata.csv`, because that field is absent from the workbook. TCGA sex, age, alcohol, smoking, prior malignancy, prior treatment, race and ethnicity come from the canonical local `ref_outs/TCGA/surgery_clinical_associations/reference/clinical_cbioportal.tsv` snapshot. TCGA stage, grade, location, nodal burden and preoperative treatment use the corrected workbook. The complete field-to-source mapping is exported in `tables/metadata_source_audit.csv`. Unknown or conflicting curated values remain missing; no alternative source fills them.

The 38 OCCAMS and 19 TCGA variables follow the two previous clinical-association scripts. Age is split at greater than 50 years (<=50 versus >50). OCCAMS performance status is 0 versus 1–2; positive-node burden is 0, 1–3 or at least 4; grades are well/moderate versus moderate/poor; Mandard groups are TRG1–3 versus TRG4–5, as explicitly requested, alongside the five separate TRG grades; response is CR/PR versus SD/PD. TCGA grade is G1/G2 versus G3/G4, pN is N0 versus N+, and smoking is 0, greater than 0–20, or greater than 20 pack-years. Explicit AJCC stages 1–4 are retained. T subcategories collapse to T0–T4. Treatment and specimen variables retain their named clinical timepoint. Multiple distinct tokens within one source field are treated as conflicting rather than selecting the first.

## Cohort membership

The five OCCAMS selections contain 225 combined neoadjuvant/surgery-pathway patients, 157 typed neoadjuvant patients, 77 neoadjuvant biopsy specimens, 80 neoadjuvant surgery specimens and 68 initial curative-intent surgery-pathway patients. They retain the previous exact three organ/site exclusions and matched-RNA requirement. The surgery pathway retains unknown specimen-source patients. TCGA includes the full surgery pathway (86) and surgery-Yes subset (72). Membership must exactly equal the historical selected-cohort membership CSV. Cohorts overlap and are not independent validation datasets.

The parent populations remain the 281 OCCAMS patients passing `Annotation_Compatible_Below_50_Flag == no` and 88 TCGA primary EAC patients. Clinical comparisons do not filter on survival endpoint availability. Thus OCCAMS surgery clinical plots can include all 68 patients while survival models include 67.

## Molecular measurements and caching

The input `ref_outs/clinical_malignant_survival/intermediate/analysis_cache.rds` supplies current malignant-expression GSVA scores and state fractions. Its upstream input hashes must still match. Both expression compartments were scored with Gaussian GSVA on common-gene `log2(CPM+1)` using the full 281/88 parent populations. This script neither changes normalization nor reruns GSVA in selected subgroups. It never consumes historical whole-bulk scores.

The fixed panel has 32 features: 17 current MPs, five state MP-gene unions, five top-20-marker DGE signatures and five biological-state within-malignant RNA fractions. Fractions retain all eight malignant labels in the denominator and are displayed as percentages. They estimate RNA contributions, not directly measured cell proportions. MP12 and the stress-adaptive MP union are identical definitions, not independent evidence. Gene-set and state exclusions follow the current centred refined contract.

Persistent outputs occupy `ref_outs/clinical_malignant_associations/{intermediate,tables,figures,reports,logs}/`. The cache contains metadata, membership, source mappings, scores, plot data and statistical results. Its contract includes input hashes, package versions, variable definitions and analytical function bodies. Changed contracts stop; `SCREF_FORCE_REBUILD=TRUE` explicitly rebuilds. `SCREF_REPLOT_ONLY=TRUE` requires a matching cache and redraws figures without statistical recalculation. A graphical change alone does not invalidate analytical data. The scientific workflow does not source temporary session checks.

## Tests and reporting

Clinical categories with fewer than three patients in a cohort are excluded from both the plots and tests, as explicitly requested. At least two retained categories are required. All retained categories enter the rank test and can receive FDR stars. Original category counts, retained categories and the number excluded by this rule remain in `tables/variable_inventory.csv`. This rule applies to clinical association categories only; it does not change survival models or patient metadata. Missing/conflicting values remain missing. Constant scores are mathematically unestimable and receive no P value. Small retained samples can affect the precision of the asymptotic rank tests.

At the requested age cutoff, the OCCAMS surgery pathway contains 68 patients aged over 50 and none aged at most 50; therefore it has no age comparison. The other six cohorts retain both age categories. Separate Mandard plots show only grades meeting the same three-patient rule, alongside TRG1–3 versus TRG4–5. The surgery pathway has no recorded Mandard scores.

Two categories use the two-sided Wilcoxon rank-sum test with normal approximation and continuity correction. Three or more categories use Kruskal–Wallis. Constant features are explicitly unestimated. Unexpected test warnings or nonfinite P values stop execution.

Figure stars use the original Benjamini–Hochberg correction within cohort, clinical variable and signature family: 17 MP tests or five tests in each state family. One, two and three stars indicate family q below 0.05, 0.01 and 0.001. There are no exact P/q or NS labels on the figures. No star can indicate either an unestimated comparison or a family q of at least 0.05; `test_eligible` in the inventory and `status` in the statistics table distinguish these cases. Tables retain both `q_family` and the broader fixed 32-feature `q`; neither controls searching across all clinical variables and overlapping cohort views. All associations are unadjusted and exploratory. No clinical ordering or cutpoint is chosen using molecular association results.

Each cohort has one MP PDF and one State PDF containing every eligible clinical variable, with one variable per page. Sex, age and the other variables are not separated into different files. The two surgery cohorts are in `Auto_final`; the other five cohorts are in `Auto_supporting`, giving 14 PDFs without duplicated pages. Full MP descriptions are wrapped and angled on a shared x axis, following `tcga_surgery_clinical_associations.R`. Coloured group boxplots, small coloured jittered points and FDR stars follow the supplied original clinical plots and the selected-survival specimen boxplot. There are no state-membership colours, per-feature panels, explanatory subtitles or footnotes. Group legends retain patient counts. State pages have three rows for MP-union scores, DGE scores and within-malignant RNA percentages, with separate y-scales. Boxes show median/interquartile range and points represent patients. Plot text uses the original restrained style, adjusted only enough to remain legible at the exported size. Methods and limitations remain in reports.

Post-treatment, recurrence and response variables are descriptive associations at their recorded timepoint, not prospective baseline prediction or causal effects. Deconvolution uncertainty is not propagated into the tests. Sparse state fractions, residual tumour purity and treatment confounding limit biological interpretation.

## Final Siewert grouping

The combined anatomical comparison uses `Site_Final` and `Siewert_Final`: oesophageal lower/middle sites and GOJ Siewert I form Oesophagus + GOJ I; GOJ Siewert II/III form GOJ II/III. This replaces the GOJ-only I versus II/III comparison. The separate I/II/III analysis remains. GOJ cases without a recorded class and unknown sites are not assigned to either group. Oesophageal cases enter the first group by recorded site without assigning a Siewert class. In the 281-patient OCCAMS parent metadata, all 239 recorded final classes occur in `Site_Final == GOJ`; the 34 oesophageal-site records have no final Siewert class. This is an observed metadata convention, not a general anatomical assertion: Siewert type I includes distal oesophageal junction-region tumours. See the [RCPA oesophagus reporting protocol](https://www.rcpa.edu.au/Library/Practising-Pathology/Structured-Pathology-Reporting-of-Cancer/Cancer-Protocols/Gastrointestinal/Elements-oesophagus). No class is inferred from oesophageal location.

## Dedicated neoadjuvant specimen comparison

`figures/Auto_supporting/occams_neoadjuvant_biopsy_vs_surgery_boxplots.pdf` reproduces the selected-survival specimen comparison layout using deconvoluted malignant GSVA scores. It contains two pages: all 17 MPs with full descriptions, then the five MP-union and five DGE state signatures in separate rows. The 77 biopsy and 80 surgery patients are independent groups. Scores retain the fixed 281-patient OCCAMS parent normalization; they are not rescored within specimen groups. Existing two-sided unpaired Wilcoxon tests and within-family BH adjustments for `occams_neo/rna_specimen_source` supply the stars. No subtitles are drawn. State RNA fractions are excluded from this dedicated GSVA comparison. Exact plotted data and tests are saved in `tables/specimen_boxplot_source_data.csv` and `tables/specimen_boxplot_statistics.csv`.

Set `SCREF_REPLOT_ONLY=TRUE,SCREF_SPECIMEN_ONLY=TRUE` on the existing launcher to export only this PDF while refreshing the source tables, index and reports. The matching clinical-association cache is required.
