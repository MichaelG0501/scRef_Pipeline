# GSE19417 tumour-channel deconvolution

## Scope and feasibility

GSE19417 is the microarray cohort used in the legacy integrated survival analysis. It contains 76 profiled cancers: 52 oesophageal adenocarcinomas, 16 gastric adenocarcinomas and eight squamous cancers. All 52 EAC specimens enter preparation and deconvolution, without treatment or surgery subgrouping; 48 have usable OS in the existing public metadata. Excluding different histologies preserves the OAC study population rather than selecting on outcome.

The processed GEO VALUE is a log10 tumour/reference ratio and is unsuitable as an absolute mixture. The original supplementary files also contain INTENSITY1 and INTENSITY2. GEO channel 1 is universal reference RNA; channel 2 is tumour RNA. The preparation explicitly confirms these channel labels and tabulates log10(INTENSITY2/INTENSITY1) against the recorded RATIO. Their correlation must be finite and positive as a channel-orientation check. Median, 99th-percentile and maximum discrepancies are retained; exact equality is not assumed between the reported intensities and the processed ratio. No patient is excluded on this diagnostic. It uses INTENSITY2 directly, never exponentiated tumour/reference ratios. The original files remain unchanged.

BayesPrism developers describe microarray mixture applications as experimental, with strong cross-platform differences requiring validation: https://github.com/Danko-Lab/BayesPrism/issues/96. Recovery of tumour-channel intensities establishes technical feasibility, not biological validation. Preparation, inference and scoring have completed; four-dataset synthesis is available as exploratory cross-platform evidence, alongside RNA-seq-only leave-one-out sensitivity estimates. Numerical completion does not validate biological state resolution.

## Inputs and preparation

All download/processing/inference scripts live in `analysis/GEO/`. Download URLs are the exact sample supplementary URLs from `ref_outs/geo_survival/Auto_GSE19417_meta.csv`, plus the original GPL4372 annotation and clinical supplement. The sample inventory and endpoint mapping derive from the public GEO metadata prepared by `analysis/clinical/geo_survival_data_prep.R`; no new clinical values or treatment histories are invented. The current downloader records source URLs, sizes and SHA256 hashes and validates gzip integrity. Existing files are reused only with integrity checks; recorded hashes must match on repeat runs. Curl has a 20-second connection limit and 600-second total limit, with no retry or alternate source. Four concurrent transfers are used.

EAC intensity matrices require identical ordered probe identifiers. Probes with a missing, nonfinite or negative tumour intensity in any EAC specimen are excluded globally before normalisation; no patient is removed and no value is imputed. The number of incomplete probes is recorded in array_qc.csv. Retained intensities must be finite and nonnegative, with positive column sums. Linear quantile normalisation is performed across the complete 52-specimen EAC parent population. GPL4372 symbols with ambiguous multi-gene annotations are explicitly excluded; missing symbols are excluded. Multiple probes for an unambiguous gene are collapsed by their median normalised intensity. Probe decisions, raw intensity totals, zero fractions and channel-ratio reconstruction errors are persisted. At least 1,000 genes must remain. No outcome-based gene or sample filter is applied.

## Exploratory inference

The current corrected scATLAS reference bundle is reused unchanged, including its strict normal-epithelial definition and malignant-unassigned category. Its source fingerprints and supported epithelial sources are verified in the allocated compute job. Shared genes require positive cohort intensity and pass the same default bulk-outlier rule as the RNA-seq comparator: relative abundance above 0.01 in more than 10% of specimens is excluded. At least 1,000 genes and five genes per final MP must remain.

Each normalised microarray column is divided by its total intensity and scaled to 1e6 for numerical inference. This is an explicit artificial relative-abundance scale, not a reconstruction of molecule or sequencing counts. No integer rounding is used. BayesPrism uses the fixed pseudobulk reference with input.type=GEP, key=Malignant, seed 22062026 and 16 cores. All eight malignant-state RNA contributions and the malignant expression matrix are retained. Fractions must be finite and nonnegative, total fractions and within-malignant fractions must sum to one, every patient must match and each reconstructed profile must have positive total expression.

The arbitrary mixture scale affects posterior precision. Posterior CV is a conditional numerical diagnostic, not calibrated uncertainty for microarray measurement noise. Probe affinities, cross-hybridisation, dynamic range, quantile normalisation and RNA-seq/reference platform mismatch can bias inferred abundance and expression. A converged fit alone does not validate transferability. Any downstream GEO-inclusive synthesis must remain exploratory and be compared with the RNA-seq-only result. Biological state fractions describe RNA contributions rather than directly measured cell proportions.

## Execution and outputs

1. `geo_microarray_download.py`: dmtcp, one CPU, 4 GB, two hours.
2. `geo_microarray_prepare.R`: dmtcp, one CPU, 8 GB, 30 minutes.
3. `geo_microarray_deconvolution.R`: dmtcp, 16 CPUs, 96 GB, six hours.

Launchers mirror these names in `job_scripts/GEO/`; PBS logs are in the project root. Stable outputs are under `ref_outs/GEO/{reference,intermediate,tables,reports,logs}/`. Persistent raw downloads, probe maps, normalised linear intensities, full fitted object and extracted expression/fractions are sufficient for reproducible downstream scoring without temporary session scripts. Preparation is deterministic and updates current outputs. Inference reuses only a matching input/settings contract; `SCREF_FORCE_REBUILD=TRUE` rebuilds. Plot-only mode is rejected because these steps do not plot.

The final canonical MPs, state unions and DGE signatures must use the same definitions as the existing RNA-seq studies. Coverage, inferred-state resolution and reference reconstruction must be reviewed before inclusion; no patient or signature may be removed because its survival direction disagrees with an expectation. After inference, geo_microarray_scores.R uses Gaussian GSVA on log2(relative malignant expression per million + 1), scored once over all 52 EAC specimens, with the current 17 MPs, five unions, five top-20 DGE signatures and five within-malignant RNA fractions. Coverage must retain at least five genes per expression signature. Its persistent scoring cache and source tables feed the explicitly requested GEO-inclusive meta-analysis. geo_microarray_quality.R renders the same three-panel initial/final malignant fraction, conditional posterior CV and eight-state composition diagnostic as GSE_Krish; the CV is explicitly conditional on the artificial mixture scale. Scoring uses dmtcp, four CPUs, 24 GB and one hour; quality plotting uses one CPU, 8 GB and 30 minutes. Both launchers mirror their R filenames. The GEO-inclusive integration and statistical/text-layout checks are complete. The final continuous MP and state pages have been visually reviewed. GEO inferred fractions require caution: squamous-to-intestinal, glandular-to-intestinal and immune-mimicry fractions are below 0.1% in 51, 45 and 41 of 52 specimens, respectively. All patients and features remain included; these descriptive diagnostics are not filtering thresholds.
